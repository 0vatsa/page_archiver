What to archive

By default every page you spend time on is archived. Narrow it down here.

Only bookmarked pages
Skip pages that aren't in your bookmarks. New bookmarks are archived right away.
Skip home pages
Skip pages like example.com/ but keep example.com/article. Sites in your list below use their own setting.

Site list

    How these rules combine
    1. If Only bookmarked pages is on and the page isn't bookmarked, it's skipped.
    2. On an allow list, only listed sites are archived (their subdomains too).
    3. On a block list, listed sites are skipped — unless the site is set to Home page only, in which case only its home page is skipped.
    4. For sites not in your list, Skip home pages skips example.com/ but keeps deeper pages.
    5. Everything else is archived.

    Manual “Archive now” follows the same rules.

    About

    Visited means saved.

    The web isn't a library. Links rot, pages change, and whole sites disappear when nobody pays the hosting bill. Page Archiver keeps your own copy of the pages you actually read — the exact page as it appeared in your browser — as a self-contained .mhtml file on your disk.

    No cloud service, no third-party archive. Your copies stay on your computer, under your control.

    Privacy

    Captures read what's already loaded in your browser. No extra requests are made to the site, and nothing leaves your computer.
